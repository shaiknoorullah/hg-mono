Annotation: put the items backOwner decision (1 October): after an unpaid order is cancelled, expires or fails payment, the app offers "Put these items back in your cart". It re-adds each line with the existing cart operations (addCartLine with the same item, variant, add-ons, quantity and note) and opens the cart; the server prices them again. A line that can't be added (sold out, removed, restaurant closed) is listed in the cart with its own error state, as on the Cart canvas. No contract change.
