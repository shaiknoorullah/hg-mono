Before a restaurant appears on HalalGoes, one of our team checks its halal certificate against seven checks. All seven must pass.

  1. 1 The certificate is legible and completeReadable, whole and unaltered.
  2. 2 The certifying body is on our accepted list
  3. 3 The legal name matches the restaurant
  4. 4 The premises address matches
  5. 5 The dates are valid todayWith time left on them. Our system checks this; no one can override it.
  6. 6 The scope covers everything soldThe whole restaurant or its kitchen, not just a supplier.
  7. 7 The certificate is not used by another restaurantOur system checks this; no one can override it.
What you see

On each restaurant page, View certification shows the certifier, the certificate number, the date it is valid until and the date we checked it. When a certificate lapses, the restaurant stops taking orders until it is renewed.

HalalGoes does not itself certify food. Certifying bodies certify food; we check their certificates.
