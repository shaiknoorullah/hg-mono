Halal edge states on cards

1. Halal field missing (client error)

RestaurantCard.halal is required. If it is missing: no badge, no certifier line, no halal words in the name; the client reports HALAL_DISPLAY_STATE_MISSING. Tapping opens Restaurant — certification unavailable.

2. Expired and unverified are never listed

Only certified and expiring-soon restaurants reach customers (halal display rules, rule 1). Feed, search, browse and favourites exclude the others, and detail returns 404 (Restaurant — not available). A customer meets an expired certificate only on a cart they already had (Cart — certification lapsed), in the slate seal below.

3. Certificate scope

At launch only certificates covering the whole restaurant or its whole kitchen pass the certificate-scope check. Certificates that cover only some menu items or only the supply chain are rejected, so no restaurant with them reaches customers and no per-item covered or uncovered marking is drawn. Item-level halal data comes in a later version.

4. Item tagged halal certified

The platform adds HALAL_CERTIFIED to MenuItem.dietary_tags. It is dropped before dietary tags render: the item shows its other tags only, and halal appears only as the restaurant HalalBadge.
