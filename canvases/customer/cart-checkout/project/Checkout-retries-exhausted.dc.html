Needs API: payment attempt limit Spec 02-customer payments rule 7: a FAILED payment may be retried on the same order up to 3 times within 15 minutes; after that the order fails for good. The contract has no error code, attempts_remaining field or order state for this, so the client cannot tell this apart from Checkout — payment declined. Drawn so the gap is visible; not buildable until the contract adds it. Before the limit: Checkout — payment declined may show "1 try left" once the contract carries attempts_remaining (also Needs API). Owner decision (1 October): after an unpaid order is cancelled, expires or fails payment, "Put these items back in your cart" re-adds each line with the existing cart operations (addCartLine: same item, variant, add-ons, quantity and note) and opens the cart, where the server prices them again. A line that can't be added shows its own cart state (sold out, removed from the menu, restaurant closed). No contract change. While the items go back, if that fails, and if the cart already holds another restaurant: Checkout — order cancelled, frames 2 to 4 (Putting your items back; We couldn't put your items back, Try again; Replace them or Keep my cart).
