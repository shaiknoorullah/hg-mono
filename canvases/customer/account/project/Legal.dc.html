How we check halal certificates

The AppBar keeps a short title that never truncates, even at 360 wide or 200% text; the full name wraps here as the first heading.

Restaurants on HalalGoes send us their halal certificate. A person on our team checks it before the restaurant is listed, and every new or renewed certificate is checked the same way. Only restaurants with a certificate we've checked are listed.

What we check

  1. The certificate is readable, complete and not altered.
  2. It comes from a certifying body on our accepted list.
  3. The name on it matches the restaurant's registered name.
  4. The address on it matches the restaurant's premises.
  5. It has been issued and has not run out.
  6. It covers the food the restaurant sells on HalalGoes.
  7. The same certificate isn't being used by another restaurant.

All seven must pass. Two of them, the dates and the reuse check, are worked out by our system and can't be overridden by anyone.

What we don't do

We don't decide whether food is halal. That is the certifying body's role. We check that a valid certificate from an accepted body is in place.

When a certificate runs out, we take the restaurant off HalalGoes until a new certificate is checked. That means we can't currently vouch for it, not that the food isn't halal.

If a certificate is close to its end date, its badge shows the date, like "Halal certified · expires 20 Oct".

If some certificate details are missing or don't load, we don't show the Halal certified badge. We say the certificate details are unavailable instead of guessing.

Owner decision (question 6, certificate details missing or not loading): when the halal panel fails to load, or the certificate record is partial (no certifier or no expiry), the restaurant shows no badge and a neutral 'certificate details unavailable' line, and the customer can still add items. This sentence replaces the earlier approved 'we show nothing rather than guess', which came before that decision. Wording approved by the owner as drafted. The badge reads 'Halal certified', never 'verified halal'. Badge states are drawn by the Discover team with HalalBadge. Contract: EXPIRED and UNVERIFIED restaurants return 404 on every customer read, so only CERTIFIED and EXPIRING_SOON ever show.
