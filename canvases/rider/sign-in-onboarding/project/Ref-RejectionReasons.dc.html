Why a document was turned down, and what the rider does next

The 'Why' line on Fix documents. The reviewer's note is always shown verbatim underneath. The remedy comes from the code: a mismatch is usually a typo in the rider's details, not a bad photo. The remedy is the card's one primary; Send for review again appears only once a fix is made.

SUSPECTED_ALTERATIONIt looks edited or changedTake a new photo
INCOMPLETE_PAGESPages or corners are missingTake a new photo
OTHERSee the reviewer noteTake a new photo (no Why line; the note is the reason)

Badge on the Fix card: derived from the remedy

  • PLATE_MISMATCH → Check your plate
  • NAME_MISMATCH, DOB_MISMATCH → Check your details
  • SUSPECTED_FORGERY → Call support
  • Every other code (ILLEGIBLE, WRONG_DOCUMENT_TYPE, EXPIRED and the rest) → New upload needed

A *_MISMATCH fix is two steps because submitRiderDocuments resends only changed documents: a details-only fix answers 422 NOTHING_TO_RESUBMIT (rider spec, documents). See Fix — nothing changed.
