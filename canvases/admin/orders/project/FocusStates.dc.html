What :focus-visible looks like on these screens, per docs/decisions/focus-indicator.md: the theme's focus colour ({{ringName}}). Borderless controls get a 2px page-coloured gap and a 3px ring. Bordered fields use their own 2px border and no ring. An invalid field keeps its danger border and gains the ring. The components draw this themselves; the rings here are depictions.
