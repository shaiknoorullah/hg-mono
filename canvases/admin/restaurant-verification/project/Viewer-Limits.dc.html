Zoom runs from 25% to 400%. At a limit its button turns off (aria-disabled, still focusable) and its name says why.

The page is a focusable region: Tab reaches it, arrow keys pan, Page Up and Page Down change page. At high zoom it scrolls instead of clipping, so every part of the certificate can be read for H1 Legible and complete.
