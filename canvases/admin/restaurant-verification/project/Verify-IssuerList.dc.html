How the list behaves

Options come from GET /v1/admin/halal-issuing-bodies with every status, Accepted first, then by name. The filter matches the name and its aliases.

Any body can be saved in the transcription. With a Rejected, Suspended or Retired body, the server suggests Issuer accepted as Fail and Reject… opens with Issuer not accepted. With a Proposed body, Reject is off: release your claim and the application waits on the certifying body.

After a proposal returns 201, the new Proposed body is chosen here for you (HCCO, highlighted).

Keyboard: focus stays in the field. Typing filters; Down and Up move the highlight (aria-activedescendant names it), Enter chooses it, Escape closes the list and keeps what you typed. Screen readers hear the option’s name, its status badge and whether it passes.

Status badges never use red: Rejected and Suspended are neutral, like Retired.
