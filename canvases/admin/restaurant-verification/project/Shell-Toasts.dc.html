After each decision

A design system Toast confirms what happened, and the page moves focus to the next useful place. Toasts never take focus themselves. Success tint is for approvals only; every reject, revoke and request for changes uses the neutral toast, because a green tick on a negative halal outcome reads as a positive halal signal. Decision toasts last 10 seconds, and their text is mirrored in a role=status line on the page, so the “sent word for word” confirmation doesn’t vanish.

Focus lands on: the queue’s Take next button.
Focus lands on: the Decision heading, which now shows the record.
Focus lands on: the Decision heading.
Focus lands on: the next document still in review, or the Decide heading if none is left.
Focus lands on: the next document still in review.
Focus lands on: the success banner on the certificate, then Back to the application.
Focus lands on: the “still needs a decision” alert on the status panel, then Request changes… in the decision row.
Focus lands on: the status panel heading.
Focus lands on: the proposal status under Issuing body.
