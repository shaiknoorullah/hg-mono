Step-up and re-sign-in: their states

Step-up cards are the body of the in-page Confirm it’s you panel. Re-sign-in cards are the body of the DS Modal (variant dialog, not dismissible): the one dialog left, because a lapsed session isn’t a working task. The fields sit in a form: Enter submits, the primary is type=submit, and autocomplete lets password managers fill them.
