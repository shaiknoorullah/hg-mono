Filled buttons carry .hg-on-brand / .hg-on-accent / .hg-on-danger, which flip the ring to white on a gap in the fill colour. The white ring measures 1.04:1 against the cream page, so it reads as a slightly bigger button. Design-system issue, not fixed on this canvas: the Button still flips its ring to white on filled buttons. The focus decision flips only where the brand ring measures under 3:1 against what it touches; here the white ring touches the cream page.

Scan region
The MultiSelect trigger is a bordered field, so its focus is its own border in --border-brand with an inset second pixel (DS .hg-field), not a ring. The viewer’s scan region is a region with aria-roledescription “document viewer” and aria-keyshortcuts (focusable, so the viewer keys only work inside it). Page thumbnails are gone until page_count exists.

Proposed components SplitPanes, folded-pane strip and Disclosure use the design system’s brand ring (brand 600, 4.30:1 on the cream page).
