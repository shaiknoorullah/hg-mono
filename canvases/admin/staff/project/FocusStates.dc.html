Keyboard focus, drawn

Rules from docs/decisions/focus-indicator.md. Left: a sidebar link and Sign out focused on the forest chrome: a chrome-coloured 2 px gap and a 3 px focus-ring-on-accent ring. Rings below are drawn statically to show the focused look.

A focused table row (.hg-row)

Staff rows are one tab stop: arrow keys move, Enter opens the account in the in-page detail panel, and closing the panel returns focus to the row. Alert rows are one tab stop too: Enter opens the alert's details beside the list; links in About are their own tab stops. Here the third row has focus: a 2 px inset offset plus a 3 px inset ring.

Focused controls

Bordered field

The field’s own border turns focus-ring and 2 px (focus-field-border-width). No outer ring on a bordered field.

Invalid field in focus

Keeps its danger border and adds the 2 px offset and 3 px ring, so focus is never carried by the error colour alone.

Radio option

The ring goes on the control of the focused option; arrow keys move between options.

Inline link in a table cell

About: Open order

Plain links use the global rule: 2 px page-colour offset and 3 px focus-ring. Uses --focus-offset-width and --focus-ring-width from the design system's tokens.css.

Panel close button

Icon buttons take the same ring. On close, focus returns to what opened the panel.
