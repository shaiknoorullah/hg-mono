Design-system issue: the orange button’s own focus ring

Primary Button (also IconButton filled and the filled Menu trigger) gives itself the on-brand focus colours: a 2px orange offset and a 3px white ring. On the cream page the white ring measures 1.05:1, on a white panel 1.00:1, so the visible change is only the 2px orange band. The focus decision gives brand 600 on cream, 4.29:1.
