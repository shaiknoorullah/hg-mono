Screen reader: what live orders says, and where focus goes

Every countdown on screen runs silent (the tile rings, the panel bar). One page announcer, with one polite and one assertive region, is the only speaker, so the same order is never announced twice.

Live regions

New order arrives
Polite: “New order A7K2, 3 items, 3 minutes to answer.” Several at once are batched: “2 new orders, most urgent B3M9, 40 seconds left.” The chime keeps ringing until each order is accepted, declined or times out.
Thresholds
This page uses 25%, 10% and 0. It drops the design system’s 50% step: 90 seconds left is not urgent in a kitchen, and fewer interruptions keep the 10% warning heard.
25% left
Polite, once per order: “B3M9, 45 seconds left.” Only the most urgent order is spoken; others queue behind it and are dropped if they pass their own threshold first.
10% left
Assertive, once per order: “B3M9, 18 seconds left to accept.” Never more than one assertive message in 5 seconds; a second one waits.
Outcome
Polite: “A7K2 timed out. The customer was not charged.” Accepted or declined on another screen: polite, with the code. The outcome text on the tile is a static note, so the announcer says it once.
Connection
Spoken once by the banner’s own region: reconnecting polite, offline assertive; signed out by its blocking alert. The lines on each tile (“Accept still works while reconnecting”) are static notes, never live.
Accepted
Polite: “A7K2 accepted, ready by 7:08 pm.” Notes from support or the rider: polite, with the code.
Never spoken
Any countdown’s own region (silent mode), per-second ticks, the once-a-second tint on the strip, list re-sorts, rider pickup-time changes.

Focus placement

New order arrives
Focus never moves. “Skip to new orders” and the Ringing badge in the status bar both jump to the strip.
In the strip
One tab stop. Left and right arrows move between orders; A accepts and D declines only the order that has focus; Enter opens it in the panel. Keys do nothing anywhere else on the page.
Panel opens
Focus moves to the panel heading (the order code). The list and the strip stay reachable; the panel is part of the page, not a dialog.
Panel closes
Close or Escape returns focus to where it came from: the tile, the list row, or the History row. If that item has gone, focus goes to the strip or list heading.
Order ends while open
Focus stays in the panel; the Accept footer is replaced by “Back to new orders”. The panel’s outcome note (alert) is the only speaker for that order.
Accept succeeds
Focus moves to the next new order in the strip; with none left, to the New orders heading.
Decline
The panel switches to the reason form, first focus on Keep order. Pressing Decline with no reason moves focus to the reason group and shows its error. Keep order returns to the tile.
Timeline pane
Opens beside the order; focus moves to its heading. Closing it returns focus to the Timeline button.
