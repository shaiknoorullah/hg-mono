What the restaurant actually receives today (table 1), what it would receive once the halal code is exposed (table 2), and the application-level codes (table 3). Labels are approved by the owner (28 September 2026); review_note, rejection.note and reason_text are always shown verbatim. The restaurant never sees the names of the seven halal checks. Nothing accuses the owner: we say what we couldn’t verify, not what they did.
