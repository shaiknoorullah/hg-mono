Orders strip: one Tab stop, a roving focus between orders

  1. Tab reaches the order list once. Focus goes to the order that expires soonest: it is a focusable group (tabindex 0, named "Order HG-10482, 3 items, 2 minutes 22 seconds left"); the other orders are tabindex -1.
  2. ← and → move focus between orders. The focused order grows to show Accept, Reject and See items; the others stay compact.
  3. A accepts the focused order, R opens the reason panel for it. The keys do nothing unless focus is inside the strip, so typing elsewhere on the page can't accept anything.
  4. Tab from the focused order goes to its Accept, Reject and See items, then to the Accepting orders switch, then out of the strip.

The ring sits on the focused order group itself (HG-10483 here), which is the element that holds focus.

Panel: where focus goes

  1. Opening a panel moves focus to its heading. The page beside it stays usable; there is no scrim and no focus trap.
  2. Close, Escape, Cancel or a finished save closes it and puts focus back on the control that opened it.
  3. If that control has gone, focus goes to the section heading, made focusable with tabindex="-1".
