Bordered fields (text input, text area, select) show focus with their own border, 2px in the focus colour, and nothing else. Borderless controls (buttons, links, cards, chips) get a 2px offset in the page colour and a 3px ring in the focus colour, which flips to white on coloured containers where it would fall below 3:1. The only double indicator is an invalid field in focus, because each mark means something different. Focus is never info blue (retired September 2026) and never a coloured glow.
