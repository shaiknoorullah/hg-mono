Six easings: standard, decelerate, accelerate, emphasized, linear, and spring (marketing only). Countdowns and progress use linear only — easing a deadline misrepresents remaining time. Reduced motion collapses every duration to 0 and cross-fades; countdown numerals keep updating.

The seal never performs. In the apps it may appear with its card, but it never animates on scroll, never pulses, never shimmers. On halalgoes.com the seal never moves at all: no stamp, pulse, shimmer or ring-draw, in any state or at any breakpoint. A moving trust mark reads as an advertisement.
