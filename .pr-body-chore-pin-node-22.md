## What
Pins Node 22 in `.nvmrc`, `engines` and `engine-strict`, points CI at that file, and makes the pre-push hook stop with the reason when the running Node is another major.

## Issue
Closes #919

## Why now
Under Node 24 seven admin tests fail and look like a real regression; the pre-push hook ran them with whatever Node the pusher's `PATH` had.
