Seven checks on every restaurant, before it reaches you.

01 · Browse

Only verified kitchens are listed.

Every restaurant on HalalGoes holds a halal certificate that a person has read against seven checks. If the certificate lapses, the listing goes with it.

02 · The record

The certificate, in the open.

Who issued it and the seven checks it passed sit on every listing. HalalGoes does not itself certify food.

03 · Sealed

Closed until you open it.

A tamper seal goes across your bag before it leaves the restaurant, and the rider scans it at pickup.

04 · At your door

Every step, on the record.

Accepted, sealed, picked up, delivered. Each step is stamped on your order in the sequence it happened.
