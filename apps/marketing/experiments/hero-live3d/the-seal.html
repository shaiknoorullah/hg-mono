How we check

02 · The document

Struck onto a record.

The seal never appears alone. It goes onto a certificate we hold on file — who issued it, which kitchen it names, what it covers, and the date it lapses. Every value here is ruled blank: no restaurant is listed yet.

03 · The seven

Seven checks, in order, by a person.

  1. 01Readable, whole, unalteredEvery page present, nothing obscured, no sign the certificate has been edited. A person looks at the scan.
  2. 02An issuer we acceptThe certifying body is on our accepted registry at the moment of review — and we print its name on the listing, so you can judge the issuer yourself.
  3. 03It names this businessThe legal name on the certificate is the restaurant's registered legal name, or a recorded alias of it. Not a parent brand.
  4. 04It covers this addressThe certified address matches the premises being listed, or the certificate names multiple premises and this is one of them.
  5. 05In force todayIssued in the past, and not expiring within our minimum remaining window. The server computes this. No admin can wave it through.
  6. 06It covers the foodThe scope has to cover the establishment or the kitchen. A certificate that only covers a meat supplier fails this check.
  7. 07Not used by anyone elseThe certificate number is not already approved for a different restaurant. Also computed by the server, also not overridable.

04 · What we don't

We don't certify food. We don't make rulings.

We do the clerical work. We get the certificate, we read it, we check it's real and still in force, and we put our name against the date we checked.

Certification verified by Halal Goes on the date shown on each listing. Halal Goes does not itself certify food.
